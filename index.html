The life of one piece

  1. 01

    Mitti the clay

    River clay, kneaded by foot and by hand until it feels alive. Nothing is poured into a mould.

  2. 02

    Chaak the wheel

    Centred, opened, raised. Ten fingers and one spinning wheel decide the shape. No two come out the same.

  3. 03

    Bhatti the fire

    A slow wood-fired kiln turns soft grey clay into strong terracotta red, ready for a long journey.

  4. 04

    Rang the finish

    Painted by hand, sealed, and checked twice before it is packed in straw and sent to you.
